Privacy policy

Your charts stay on your computer.

This page explains what 3suns, the astrology app for Mac and Windows, and 3suns.app, this website, collect, and why. It was last updated on 15 November 2026.

In short

  • The app has no account and no sign-in. Your charts, notes and reports are kept on your computer, and they are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app goes online for two things only: checking your licence key and checking for updates.
  • The website counts visits with PostHog, without cookies. You can turn that off for your browser.
  • We don't sell personal information, show ads, or share data for advertising.

Who we are

3suns is made and published by Karia LLC. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Karia LLC. You can reach us about anything on this page at hello@karia.cc.

The 3suns app

Your charts and your work

The charts you enter, your notes, chart files, settings, saved styles and the reports you make are stored in the 3suns data folder on your computer. The app does not upload them, there is no cloud copy, and nothing you enter is sent to an AI service. The interpretation text is part of the app. Places and time zones come from an atlas and a time zone database built into the app, so looking up a birth place does not go online either. Back-ups of your data are up to you, and we cannot recover data from your computer.

Your licence key

3suns uses Keygen (keygen.sh), a licensing service, to check licence keys. When you activate a key, the app sends Keygen:

  • the licence key,
  • a fingerprint of the computer: the system's hardware ID, scrambled together with the product name, so it means nothing outside 3suns,
  • the computer's name as set in its system settings, so you can tell your computers apart in the licence window,
  • the operating system and its version, for example macOS 15.1.

While the key is active, the app checks it with Keygen at most about once a day when you are online, and it keeps working offline for up to 30 days after the last check. When you move the licence to another computer, the old computer's record is deleted at Keygen. When we issue a key, we also record the name and email address it was issued to.

Updates

A few seconds after it opens, and whenever you choose Check for Updates, the app downloads a small file from GitHub, where the 3suns installers are published, to see whether a newer version exists. Updates are downloaded from GitHub too. These are plain downloads: like any download, they reveal your IP address to GitHub, but they carry nothing about you or your charts.

Nothing else

The app has no usage tracking, no crash reporting and no ads. When you open a link from your notes or from Help, it opens in your web browser, outside the app.

This website

Hosting

3suns.app is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and the details your browser sends in order to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks. The site sets no cookies. Its fonts are served from 3suns.app itself, and the live sky wheel on the home page is computed in your browser.

Visitor analytics

We use PostHog to see how people find and use the site, so we know which pages help and how many people download 3suns. When analytics is on, PostHog records:

  • the pages you view and how far down you read,
  • the links and buttons you click, including which installer you download and from where on the page,
  • the page or post that sent you here, and any campaign tags in the link you followed,
  • your browser, operating system, device type and screen size,
  • your approximate location (country and city), which PostHog works out from your IP address. PostHog keeps the IP address with the visit.

Instead of a cookie, the site keeps a random visit ID in your browser's session storage. It is deleted when you close the tab, so we see one visit at a time and don't recognise you when you come back. We don't build profiles of visitors, and we don't link visits to licence keys. The analytics requests go to our own address, 3suns.app/ingest, which passes them on to PostHog in the United States.

Analytics is on in this browser.

Downloads

The installers are hosted on GitHub. When you click a download button, your browser fetches the file from GitHub, which handles that request under its own privacy statement.

If you write to us

We use your email address and what you write only to answer you, and we keep the conversation for as long as it is useful for that.

The companies we work with

We share data only with the services that make 3suns work, and only what each one needs: Keygen for licence keys, PostHog for website analytics, Cloudflare for hosting the website, and GitHub for downloads and updates. Each handles data under its own terms and may process it in the United States. We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to hello@karia.cc. Deleting your licence record ends the licence on your computer. Website visits are tied only to a random ID, so we may not be able to find a single visit, but we will help where we can. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR, and we will honour them.

Children

3suns is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information about them.

Changes to this policy

If what 3suns collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
