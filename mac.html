Astrology software, at home on your Mac.

Native builds for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, notarized by Apple, with every chart kept on your Mac and new versions arriving inside the app.

In short

  • Two native builds: one for Apple Silicon (M1 and later) and one for Intel Macs, on macOS 11 Big Sur or later.
  • Signed by Karia LLC and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other Mac app.
  • Your charts stay on your Mac. No account. The app goes online only to check its licence key and to look for updates, and it keeps working offline for up to 30 days at a time.
  • Updates arrive inside the app: the new version downloads in the background and installs when you restart or quit.

Which version is yours

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it shows a Chip, such as Apple M1 or later, download the Apple Silicon version. If it shows a Processor by Intel, download the Intel version. Not sure? Choose Apple Silicon, the chip in most Macs from 2021 on. Both are on the download page.

Mac and Windows

3suns also runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11, with the same features. A licence key works on one computer at a time and moves to another from the licence window, and your charts move with you as AAF, CSV or JSON.

Questions

Does 3suns run natively on Apple Silicon?

Yes. The Apple Silicon version is built for M-series chips, and the Intel version for Intel Macs. Pick the one that matches your Mac.

Which versions of macOS does it need?

macOS 11 Big Sur or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Calculations, the atlas and the time zone history are all on your Mac. The app checks its licence key about once a day when you are online and keeps working offline for up to 30 days after the last check.

How do updates work?

3suns looks for a new version shortly after it starts and every few hours. When there is one, it downloads in the background and installs when you restart or quit the app.

Is there an iPad or iPhone app?

Not yet. 3suns runs on Mac and Windows computers, including Windows tablets such as Microsoft Surface.
