Coming from Solar Fire

Your Solar Fire charts, opened directly.

3suns reads Solar Fire chart files as they are, on Mac and Windows. Notes, Rodden ratings and life events come along, and every chart keeps the time zone Solar Fire used.

In short

  • 3suns opens Solar Fire chart files (.SFcht) as they are. There is nothing to export or convert first, and Solar Fire does not need to be installed.
  • Notes, Rodden ratings and life events come along with each chart, and so do its house system and zodiac, tropical or sidereal.
  • Every chart keeps the time zone Solar Fire used, so its birth moment stays exactly as you entered it.
  • It works the same on Mac (macOS 11 Big Sur or later) and Windows 10 or 11. Your Solar Fire files are only read, never changed.

Move your charts in four steps

  1. Find your chart files. Solar Fire usually keeps them in Documents › Solar Fire User Files › Charts. One .SFcht file can hold hundreds of charts.
  2. Copy them to the computer with 3suns, if that is another computer: a USB stick, a cloud folder or an email to yourself all work.
  3. Choose Import charts on the start page of 3suns and pick your .SFcht files.
  4. Check the count. 3suns says how many charts came in and how many were already there, so importing a file twice adds no duplicates.

What comes across

Each chart arrives ready to use, with what Solar Fire stored for it:

  • Name, date, time, place and coordinates
  • The time zone offset Solar Fire applied, summer time included
  • The Rodden rating, shown beside the birth data
  • Notes, kept with the chart
  • Life events, shown above the wheel
  • House system and zodiac, including a sidereal zodiac and its ayanamsa

Your program settings, page designs and report texts stay in Solar Fire. Set your preferences in 3suns once, and they apply to every chart.

Charts from other programs

Charts from astro.com, Janus and many other programs come in as AAF, either as a file or as pasted text. A list kept in a spreadsheet comes in as CSV, and JSON works too. Every chart file exports back to AAF, CSV or JSON at any time, so your charts never get stuck in 3suns.

Once your charts are in

Everything works on the imported charts straight away: transits, progressions, solar arc and returns, synastry and composite charts, a year timeline with exact dates, primary directions, traditional techniques, astrocartography, Vedic charts and client reports with your own branding. Questions

Do I need Solar Fire on the same computer?

No. 3suns reads the chart files themselves, so you only need the .SFcht files, not the program.

Can I keep using Solar Fire alongside 3suns?

Yes. 3suns only reads your chart files and changes nothing in them. Import again whenever you like: charts that are already in 3suns are skipped.

Which files has the import been tested with?

It has been tested on 3,526 charts in 20 chart files saved by Solar Fire 9.

Does it run on Mac and Windows?

Yes. Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel on macOS 11 Big Sur or later, and 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. The import works the same on both.

Where are my charts kept after the import?

On your computer, in the 3suns chart database. You don't need an account, and your charts are never sent anywhere.
