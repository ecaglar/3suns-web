Primary directions

Primary directions, every choice open.

Six methods in zodiaco and in mundo, eight keys, direct and converse, and the speculum behind every arc, in the same window as the chart.

In short

  • Six methods: Placidus semi-arc, Placidus under the pole and Regiomontanus in zodiaco; Placidus, Regiomontanus and Campanus in mundo.
  • Eight keys: Naibod, Ptolemy, Cardan, your own rate, the Sun's motion on the birthday and the true solar arc, each in right ascension or longitude where it applies.
  • Direct and converse, with the latitude of the aspects taken on the ecliptic, from the planet, or by Bianchini's rule.
  • Significators and promissors you choose, with the Part of Fortune, antiscia and fixed stars, and the speculum behind every arc. On Mac and Windows.

Methods

MethodDirectsHow the place is measured
Placidus semi-arcin zodiacoThe fraction of a point's diurnal or nocturnal semi-arc between meridian and horizon; the promissor's degree is brought to the same fraction of its own semi-arc.
Placidus under the polein zodiacoThe significator gets the pole at which its oblique ascension matches its semi-arc place, and promissors are raised under that pole.
Regiomontanusin zodiacoThe significator's position circle through the north and south points of the horizon. Campanus uses the same circles, so its zodiacal directions are the same.
Placidus semi-arcin mundoThe promissor's body is directed, and aspects are measured in house space: a square is three houses.
Regiomontanusin mundoHouses divide the equator, so mundane aspects are counted along the equator from the significator's circle.
Campanusin mundoHouses divide the prime vertical.

Keys

A key turns the arc into years. 3suns offers eight:

  • Naibod, 0°59′08″ a year, the Sun's mean daily motion
  • Ptolemy, 1° a year
  • Cardan, 0°59′12″ a year
  • Your own rate, in degrees of arc for one year
  • The Sun's motion on the birthday, in right ascension or in longitude, as a fixed rate
  • The true solar arc, the progressed Sun's motion after birth, in right ascension or in longitude

Every other choice

  • Latitude. Aspects of the promissors on the ecliptic with no latitude, with the planet's own latitude, or by Bianchini's rule. Significators on the ecliptic, as Morinus does by default, or with their own latitude. In mundo the bodies are always directed with their latitude.
  • Significators. The Ascendant, Midheaven, Descendant and IC, the planets and the Part of Fortune.
  • Promissors. The planets and the node with their Ptolemaic aspects, the Part of Fortune, antiscia and contra-antiscia, and fixed stars by conjunction.
  • Converse directions, the sky turned backwards, and optionally timed by the Sun before birth.
  • Span. The next ten years, the last ten, birth to age 100, or any ages you set.

How the arcs are checked

Positions come from the Swiss Ephemeris. In the tests, every direction is checked by turning the sky through its arc and confirming, with the Swiss Ephemeris house position of the promissor, that it reaches the significator's place, for births in the northern and southern hemisphere. A full listing for one chart is kept as a reference, so a change in any result is caught.

Questions

Why do two programs give different dates for the same direction?

Because the conventions differ, and each one moves the date. In one test chart, the Moon's latitude alone moved directions to the Moon by 9 to 12 months, the method by about 9 months, and the key by 13 months at age 21. 3suns shows every choice, so you can match the convention you work with.

Can I export the directions?

Yes. Every direction in the list exports as CSV for a spreadsheet, with its date, age, promissor, aspect, significator, arc and whether it is direct or converse.

Does it work for any birthplace?

Anywhere outside the polar circles, where semi-arcs stop being defined. A birth time is needed.

Does it run on Mac and Windows?

Yes. Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel on macOS 11 Big Sur or later, and 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
